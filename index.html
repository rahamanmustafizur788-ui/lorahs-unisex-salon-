Frequently asked questions

Is ToolGhor really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use without registration.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Image, PDF and text processing happens in your browser. Your files are not sent to ToolGhor. See the Privacy Policy.

Do the tools work on mobile?

Yes. The site is responsive and works in modern mobile and desktop browsers.

Why does a tool say it could not load a library?

PDF and QR tools load a small open-source library from this website the first time you use them. Check your internet connection and refresh the page.
