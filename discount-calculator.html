Discount Calculator

Work out sale prices, savings and tax.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter your values.
  2. Click Calculate.
  3. Read the result below the form.

About the Discount Calculator

Find how much you save and the final price after a percentage discount, with optional tax added after the discount. Handy when comparing sale offers.

Frequently asked questions

Is tax added before or after the discount?

Here tax is applied after the discount. Rules differ by shop and country, so check your receipt.
