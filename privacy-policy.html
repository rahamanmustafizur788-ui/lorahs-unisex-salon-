Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Your files and text

Image, PDF, text and calculator tools run in your browser. Files and text you use are not uploaded to ToolGhor.

Optional accounts

You can use every tool without an account. If you create one, we store your name, email, sign-in details, the tools you use while signed in and the tools you save, using Supabase (our authentication and database provider). Row-level security means only you can read your data. You can clear your history, or ask us to delete your account via the contact page. If you use Google Sign-In, Google shares your name and email with us. Only these optional account features contact Supabase or Google.

Data we store

Your theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. The contact form simply opens your own email app; we do not store form entries on the website.

Third parties

The PDF and QR tools use open-source libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js, qrcode-generator) that are hosted on this website, so the tools themselves do not contact other servers. Our web host (Hostinger) may keep standard server logs. If advertising such as Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads; you can manage this in your Google ad settings, and we will update this policy and show any required consent notice before ads go live.
