Merge PDF

Join several PDF files into one.

How to use the Merge PDF

  1. Choose your PDF or image file(s).
  2. Set any options shown.
  3. Click the button, then download the result.

About the Merge PDF

Join several PDF files into a single document, such as combining a form and its attachments. Select two or more PDFs; they are merged in the order selected.

Frequently asked questions

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

Files that require a password to open may fail. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first.

Privacy

Your PDFs are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded.
