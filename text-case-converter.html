About the Text Case Converter

Switch text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case in one step, then copy the result. Saves retyping headings and fixing text pasted in capitals.

Frequently asked questions

Does Title Case skip small words?

No, it capitalises every word, so review headings that need style-guide exceptions.

Privacy
