Typing Speed Test

Measure your typing speed and accuracy in 60 seconds.

How to use the Typing Speed Test

  1. Fill in the options.
  2. Run the tool.
  3. Copy or download the result.

About the Typing Speed Test

Type the passage shown for 60 seconds. You get words per minute (based on 5 characters per word) and accuracy. Results vary with keyboard and practice.

Frequently asked questions

Is WPM affected by mistakes?

Yes. Only correctly typed characters count towards your speed.

Privacy

This tool runs locally in your browser; what you enter is not sent to a server.
