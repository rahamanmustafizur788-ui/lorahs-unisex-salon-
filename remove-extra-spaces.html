Remove Extra Spaces

Clean up repeated spaces and blank lines.

How to use the Remove Extra Spaces

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Results update as you type.
  3. Copy the result where needed.

About the Remove Extra Spaces

Clean text copied from PDFs or emails by collapsing repeated spaces and tabs, trimming line ends and optionally reducing multiple blank lines to one.

Frequently asked questions

Does it change my words?

No. Only spacing is adjusted.

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser and is never sent to a server.
